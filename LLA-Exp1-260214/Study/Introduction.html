Your Health Decision Assessment

In this study, we will ask you to imagine a medical scenario and make decisions about treatment options. Your choices will help us understand how people think about health-related decisions.

What to expect

  • You will be asked to imagine being diagnosed with a health condition and to consider different treatment options
  • You will indicate your preferences between treatments with different possible outcomes
  • You will answer a few questions about how you would feel while considering these options

Please note: This is a purely hypothetical scenario. There are no right or wrong answers — we are only interested in your genuine preferences. Please try to imagine the situation as vividly as you can.

By continuing, you confirm that you have read the study information and consent to participate. You may withdraw at any time.
