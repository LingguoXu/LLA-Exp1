Your Diagnosis

Please try to imagine the following situation as though it were happening to you.

Background

You are 60 years old and living in the United Kingdom. Based on national statistics, a person your age would typically expect to live to around 80 years old. You have been generally healthy throughout your life.

Diagnosis

During a routine check-up, your doctor discovers that you have a genetic condition called Beta-syndrome. This is a condition that, unfortunately, will shorten your remaining life expectancy.

Your doctor explains that with Beta-syndrome, you can expect to live anywhere between 60 and 70 years old, but no more than 70. Without any treatment, your outlook is uncertain within this range.

Your doctor reassures you that treatment options are available. On the next page, you will learn about two specific drugs that could help manage Beta-syndrome.

70
Maximum years you can live with Beta-syndrome.
Treatment may extend this further.
